# Design System: Sahil Pohare Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Manipulable Systems Field"**

The portfolio presents an engineer with a founder story through an inspectable systems field. Its visual authority comes from strict black and white, dense ASCII volumes, mathematically real dimensional projections, one-pixel construction lines, stark inversion, and compressed propositions. It should feel engineered rather than decorated: rigorous, direct, unconventional, and economical without looking cheap. It never frames Sahil as a CEO.

ASCII is functional material. It carries geometry, topology, state, coordinates, code, and transitions; it is not a costume applied to ordinary cards. A monochrome VHS/CRT carrier makes the whole page feel transmitted rather than polished into sterility, while motion reveals structure or communicates system activity. The resume remains complete and understandable when effects, motion, and canvas output are unavailable.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Strict monochrome surfaces with no chromatic accents.
- Square, one-pixel frames that join into larger spatial systems.
- Compressed uppercase display type for propositions and practical sans-serif for reading.
- Monospace reserved for characters that behave as data, geometry, code, labels, or controls.
- State communicated through text, check marks, borders, and inversion rather than color alone.
- Progressive motion that stops when offscreen and disappears under reduced-motion preferences.
- Compact section rhythm and deliberately compressed model stages keep each proof close to one viewport.
- A page-wide, pointer-transparent scanline, vignette, flicker, and tracking-displacement carrier.
- Technical practices expressed as selectable axes of a true 16-vertex, 32-edge 4D tesseract.

## Colors

The palette is a hard monochrome system: black and white carry structure and state, off-white moderates long light sections, five exact gray roles carry secondary information, and two alpha blacks build the carrier.

### Primary
- **Field Black:** The dominant dark canvas, terminal-like artifacts, selected light-surface controls, and dark-side inversion.

### Neutral
- **Ink Black:** A secondary near-black available for dense ink where pure field black is unnecessarily severe.
- **Signal White:** Primary light surface, dark-surface text, borders, focus indicators, and inverted action state.
- **Paper Off-White:** Long-form light sections that need a softer reading field without introducing hue.
- **Muted on Dark:** Supporting copy and inactive detail on black surfaces.
- **Muted on Light:** Supporting copy and explanatory text on white or off-white surfaces.
- **Inactive Gray:** Inactive navigation and unselected light-surface metadata.
- **Stream Gray:** Muted historical token rows inside black terminal artifacts.
- **Selected Muted:** Supporting labels inside selected black tabs.
- **Scanline Black:** Low-alpha black used only in the repeating CRT scanline overlay.
- **Vignette Black:** Low-alpha black used only in the radial edge vignette.

### Named Rules
**The No Accent Rule.** Do not introduce hue, component gradients, tinted glows, or green-terminal color coding. State is black, white, gray, pattern, text, or inversion.

**The Stark Inversion Rule.** Interactive emphasis flips foreground and background completely; it does not rely on subtle opacity changes.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Black (with Arial Black and sans-serif fallbacks)

**Body Font:** Public Sans (with Helvetica, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks)

**Label/Mono Font:** SFMono-Regular (with Cascadia Mono, Roboto Mono, Consolas, and monospace fallbacks)

**Character:** The display face makes propositions feel compressed, structural, and decisive. Public Sans keeps explanations practical and readable; the monospace stack marks information that belongs to the system rather than using terminal type as atmosphere.

### Hierarchy
- **Hero Display** (400, `clamp(2.6rem, 5.2vw, 4.8rem)`, 0.9): Compact uppercase identity proposition, constrained to `12ch` on wide screens; mobile resets it to `clamp(2.1rem, 8.5vw, 3rem)` and releases the width constraint.
- **Section Display** (400, `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.75rem)`, 0.92): Uppercase section propositions; mobile section headings use `clamp(2.35rem, 12vw, 3.8rem)`.
- **Feature Display** (400, `clamp(1.9rem, 3.6vw, 3.6rem)`, 0.95): Technical-focus proposition inside the black skills frame.
- **System Display** (400, `clamp(2.25rem, 4.5vw, 4.3rem)`, 0.95): Active system title.
- **Card Display** (400, `clamp(1.55rem, 2.7vw, 2.7rem)`, 1): Primary framed-card heading. Exact supporting display ranges are skill `clamp(1.25rem, 2vw, 2rem)`, artifact `clamp(1.3rem, 2.2vw, 2.1rem)`, compact `clamp(1.3rem, 2.1vw, 2rem)`, history `clamp(1.4rem, 2.4vw, 2.5rem)`, notes `clamp(1.7rem, 3vw, 3rem)`, and kitchen outcome `clamp(2.2rem, 4.5vw, 4.5rem)`.
- **Contact Display** (400, `clamp(3rem, 7vw, 5rem)`, 0.92): Final proposition; mobile uses `clamp(3rem, 15vw, 4.6rem)`.
- **Metric** (700, `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 4rem)`, 1): Tabular outcome figures; mobile fixes this at `3rem`.
- **Body** (400, `1.05rem`, 1.5): Explanations and narrative. Hero body copy uses `clamp(1rem, 1.3vw, 1.18rem)`.
- **System Labels** (400-800, `0.6-0.68rem`, usually 1-1.5): Coordinates, statuses, controls, metadata, banners, and code. Exact fixed exceptions are `0.55rem` ASCII rules, `0.7rem` links and time, `0.76rem` tab titles, `0.82rem` ledger names, `0.92rem` fact values, `1.1rem` monogram, and `1.2rem` timeline node.
- **Canvas Labels** (400-700, `9-16px`, 1): Compact canvas marks use `9px`; standard edges use `10px`; vertices and labels use `11px`; selected edges use `13px`; the MoltMesh moving glyph reaches `16px` on wide screens.
- **Narrow Mobile Mono** (400-700, `0.48-0.54rem`): `0.48rem` is reserved for dense terminal output and `0.54rem` for compact fixed navigation where the implementation cannot wrap.

The remaining exact CSS type values are preserved as named frontmatter roles: body (`1.05rem`), control label (`0.65rem`), terminal (`clamp(0.62rem, 1.15vw, 0.9rem)` at `1.65`), wide display (`clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6rem)`), code (`clamp(0.66rem, 1.1vw, 0.9rem)` at `1.7`), ASCII diagram (`clamp(0.45rem, 0.8vw, 0.7rem)`), action display (`clamp(1.4rem, 3vw, 2.8rem)`), mobile section (`clamp(2.35rem, 12vw, 3.8rem)`), mobile kitchen (`clamp(2.2rem, 9.5vw, 3.4rem)`), mobile contact (`clamp(3rem, 15vw, 4.6rem)`), mobile metric (`3rem`), mobile action (`1.4rem`), and the relative metric prefix (`0.45em`). Hidden replacement text uses `font-size: 0` only to expose generated visual text while preserving the accessible name. Together with the hierarchy above, this is the complete set of `font-size` values represented in the stylesheet.

### Named Rules
**The Three-Voice Rule.** Display asserts, body explains, and monospace proves or controls. Do not interchange these roles for novelty.

**The Data-Only Mono Rule.** Monospace must indicate geometry, code, route state, numbers, controls, or machine-like metadata; prose remains in Public Sans.

## Layout

Desktop uses a fixed vertical identity rail (`5.25rem`) and a content field offset by the same width. The compact hero retains a `100vh` minimum but limits its proposition to `clamp(2.6rem, 5.2vw, 4.8rem)` and uses only `clamp(2rem, 5vh, 4rem)` above the copy before the MoltMesh stage. Sections use fluid horizontal padding and materially compressed vertical intervals (`clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)`), with asymmetric two-column introductions that place the proposition beside a narrower explanation. Framed content forms contiguous grids: adjacent cells share one-pixel dividers rather than floating with gaps.

At `920px` and below, the identity rail becomes a fixed `3.7rem` horizontal route bar. Major split layouts collapse to one column, route tabs become horizontally scrollable, the technical-focus heading and tesseract stage stack, and the four skill axes become a two-by-two matrix. At `640px` and below, horizontal section padding becomes `1rem`, section pacing tightens to `3rem`, and action junctions, artifact primitives, diffs, tables, footer metadata, and skill axes collapse to reading-order stacks. The MoltMesh controls remain a two-column touch matrix; its two framed actions share the available width equally. The page must not create horizontal overflow. Minimum heights step from desktop to compact (`<=920px`) to narrow (`<=640px`) as follows: tesseract `26rem / 26rem / 18rem`, MoltMesh `clamp(35rem, 60vw, 48rem) / 36rem / 34rem`, and kitchen model `clamp(28rem, 40vw, 34rem) / 36rem / 33rem`. Skill-axis cells are `13rem` on desktop and `12rem` on narrow screens.

Touch and keyboard targets use a minimum height of `2.75rem` where controls or inline calls to action require it. Dense content may scroll inside a bounded control strip, never across the entire page.

### Named Rules
**The Joined-Frame Rule.** Related items meet edge to edge and share dividers. Do not separate them into rounded, floating cards.

**The Reading-Order Rule.** Responsive collapse follows semantic source order; visual geometry never makes meaning depend on desktop position.

## Elevation & Depth

The system uses no box shadows, blur, colored effects, or component-level gradient depth. Physical hierarchy comes from black/white field changes, one-pixel boundaries, scale, overlap within bounded artifacts, and ASCII character density. Canvas depth is produced by perspective projection and opacity; the tesseract explicitly projects 4D coordinates into 3D and then 2D. The only gradients are the global functional carrier: repeating black scanlines and a radial black edge vignette.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-by-Construction Rule.** Every interface surface is flat. Depth belongs to represented systems and motion, not ornamental UI elevation.

**The Carrier Exception Rule.** Gradients are prohibited in components but required for the monochrome scanline and vignette overlays; they must remain pointer-transparent and content-preserving.

**The Capture Exception Rule.** Screenshot tooling may add `capture-mode` to suppress both carrier overlays and change the fixed identity rail to absolute positioning. This is a deterministic capture accommodation, never a visitor-facing visual variant.

## Shapes

All components are square (`0px` radius). Containers, controls, tables, tabs, and artifacts use one-pixel solid rules in the current foreground color. Dashed one-pixel rules are reserved for route or history continuity. Recurring silhouettes are rectangular frames, joined cells, ASCII brackets, plus-sign registration marks, and check-state markers (`[ ]` / `[x]`).

Outlined display text uses a narrow stroke only as a typographic counterpoint. Decorative pills, soft blobs, circles, and arbitrary cut corners do not belong to this system.

### Named Rules
**The One-Pixel Grammar Rule.** Structure is drawn with one-pixel rules; thicker borders are reserved for the two-pixel focus outline, never routine framing.

## Components

### Controls and Action Rows
- **Shape:** Square cells with one-pixel boundaries and at least `2.75rem` target height.
- **Default:** Match the containing field: black with white text or white with black text.
- **Hover / Selected:** Apply complete black/white inversion. Selected controls retain semantic state through `aria-pressed`, `aria-selected`, or `[x]`, not inversion alone.
- **Focus:** Use a `2px` current-color outline offset by `4px`; preserve it over both dark and light fields.
- **Active:** No separate transform or simulated physical press is established.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** Hard square corners.
- **Background:** One of the four field surfaces; cards do not introduce local tint.
- **Depth Strategy:** No shadows. Use shared one-pixel borders, inversion, and scale.
- **Internal Padding:** Compact metadata starts around `0.8rem`; standard cells use `1.4rem`; feature panels may use fluid padding up to `5rem`.
- **Behavior:** Linked evidence panels may invert completely on hover; static evidence remains flat.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** A fixed white vertical rail with a black monogram block. Uppercase monospace labels rotate vertically and expose route state as `[ ]` or `[x]`.
- **Compact:** Below `920px`, navigation becomes a fixed horizontal bar with overflow available to route links; the contact label hides but its arrow remains.
- **State:** Inactive labels are gray, while hover and active labels become black; the check marker provides the non-color state cue.

### Tabs
- **Structure:** A contiguous framed row of monospace controls with a check marker, title, and supporting label.
- **Selected:** Black field, white title, muted light supporting text, `[x]` marker, `aria-selected="true"`, and roving `tabindex`.
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys cycle, Home selects the first tab, and End selects the last.
- **Compact:** The row scrolls horizontally instead of wrapping or shrinking labels beyond readability.

### ASCII Artifacts
- **Role:** Diagrams and terminals explain system behavior through characters, logs, frames, and explicit labels.
- **Frame:** One-pixel rectangular boundary with a compact monospace status strip.
- **Meaning:** Every visual artifact requires nearby semantic text or an accessible label. Purely duplicated ASCII and canvas marks remain hidden from assistive technology.
- **State:** Character density, labels, and inversion carry focus; no color legend is required.

### Interactive Canvas
- **Rendering:** Resize to the visible container and cap the backing-store device-pixel ratio at `2` to bound memory and raster cost.
- **Animation:** Use `requestAnimationFrame`; pause when the canvas leaves the viewport or the document is hidden, and cancel stale frames before restarting.
- **Reduced Motion:** Render a static frame, disable automatic rotation and traveling glyphs, but preserve direct layer selection and pointer-driven inspection.
- **Accessibility:** Treat canvas as an enhancement (`aria-hidden="true"`). Expose title, explanation, instructions, and layer controls as semantic HTML with pressed states.

### Technical-Focus Tesseract
- **Geometry:** Generate all `16` vertices from four binary dimensions and all `32` edges by connecting vertices that differ along exactly one dimension.
- **Projection:** Rotate multiple planes, then project 4D to 3D and 3D to 2D. W, X, Y, and Z edge families map to the four technical-practice controls.
- **State:** Selecting an axis updates `aria-pressed`, the textual readout, the inverted control, and the corresponding edge family. Active edges use alternating `#` and `=` characters; inactive edges use `.` and `+`.
- **Rendering:** Use an opaque black canvas, white characters, depth-sensitive alpha, capped device-pixel ratio (`2`), resize redraws, viewport pausing, and document-visibility pausing.
- **Reduced Motion:** Freeze rotational time at a deterministic projection while preserving button selection and immediate redraw.
- **Accessibility:** Keep the canvas hidden from assistive technology and place the title, projection description, current-axis readout, practice descriptions, technology lists, and pressed controls in HTML.

### MoltMesh Protocol Volume
- **Geometry:** Build four labeled point spheres joined by five protocol edges; character density and depth alpha form the volume, while a task glyph travels along real edges.
- **Interaction:** Pointer position and dragging rotate the model. Five pressed-state controls isolate all layers, signed identity, delegation, microVM isolation, or replicated consensus and update the adjacent semantic readout.
- **Actions:** End the stage with two equal, one-pixel framed actions for `openmolt.network` and GitHub. Neither action is visually subordinate.
- **Rendering:** Use the shared interactive-canvas performance, reduced-motion, and accessibility contract.

### Hybrid Kitchen Cluster Model
- **Geometry:** Render a perspective floor grid and five cuboid infrastructure objects for the kitchen counter, physical home Kubernetes node, free Azure control plane, PAYG pods, and Vercel/serverless path.
- **Topology:** Draw separate Cloudflare Tunnel ingress, Azure control, on-failure failover, and transaction-path links; selected paths use denser ASCII and carry a moving task glyph when motion is allowed.
- **Interaction:** Drag to orbit or use five pressed-state controls to isolate the kitchen node, tunnel, control plane, downtime pods, or serverless critical path while updating the semantic readout.
- **Rendering:** Use the shared interactive-canvas performance, reduced-motion, and accessibility contract.

### VHS/CRT Carrier
- **Construction:** Two fixed full-viewport body pseudo-elements at `z-index: 90`, both with `pointer-events: none`.
- **Scanlines:** A repeating vertical rhythm of three transparent pixels followed by one low-alpha black line, multiplied over the page at `0.42` opacity.
- **Vignette:** A radial black edge shade beginning after `58%` of the viewport radius and reaching the edge at `100%`.
- **Motion:** Raster opacity flickers briefly in a `7s` stepped cycle; a narrow clipped band shifts by `-3px` and `2px` during `92-94%` of an `11s` tracking cycle.
- **Reduced Motion:** Cancel the animated carrier entirely. Static scanlines and vignette may remain because they do not encode meaning or move.
- **Capture Mode:** Screenshot tooling may remove both overlays and unfix the identity rail so full-page captures are stable and free of repeated fixed artifacts.

### Scroll Reveals
- **Behavior:** Reveal once on entry, then unobserve. Decorative ASCII banners scan from left, framed diagrams compress into focus, and repeated evidence rises in short staggered groups. Titles and headings are never reveal-animation targets and remain fully rendered throughout.
- **Motion:** `650ms` with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; repeated items stagger by `70ms`, capped at three increments.
- **Safety:** Add the motion-ready class only after targets are prepared to avoid hiding content when JavaScript fails. Under reduced motion, remove opacity, clipping, transform, and filter effects.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use black/white inversion as the primary interactive response.
- **Do** join related modules with shared one-pixel dividers and preserve their structural relationships while stacking.
- **Do** use ASCII to communicate topology, state, sequence, code, or measured behavior.
- **Do** preserve visible focus, keyboard tab behavior, semantic state, and meaningful content without canvas or motion.
- **Do** pause continuous canvas work offscreen and cap raster resolution.
- **Do** build dimensional diagrams from real topology and expose their axis state in accessible HTML controls.
- **Do** keep the VHS/CRT carrier monochrome, restrained, fixed, and pointer-transparent.
- **Do** reserve capture mode for deterministic screenshot tooling; keep it out of normal interaction and production state.
- **Do** position Sahil as an engineer with a founder story; keep founder judgment primary and technical depth as evidence.
- **Do** keep route-specific story, proof order, and artifact content in the relevant surface brief.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce component gradients, chromatic accents, green-terminal nostalgia, glows, glass effects, or shadows; the monochrome carrier gradients are the sole established exception.
- **Don't** use rounded cards, pills, floating panels, generic project-card grids, or decorative dashboard widgets.
- **Don't** set explanatory prose in monospace or use ASCII as an empty decorative texture.
- **Don't** communicate selection, activity, or hierarchy through color or animation alone.
- **Don't** animate hidden canvas work, replay scroll reveals, or leave content concealed when JavaScript or motion is unavailable.
- **Don't** replace the tesseract with arbitrary connected points or a decorative pseudo-3D approximation.
- **Don't** let scanlines, vignette, flicker, or tracking displacement intercept input, obscure copy, encode state, or survive as motion under reduced-motion preferences.
- **Don't** describe Sahil as a CEO or animate any title or heading as a reveal target.
- **Don't** promote this portfolio's route-specific narrative or exact section composition into a global design-system rule.
